Frequently Answered Questions

How can I track my hardware shipment?
Every confirmed order generates a unique Order ID (e.g. #GT-8492). You can monitor real-time courier fulfillment directly in your Account Portal or enter your order number on the invoice lookup screen.
What is the GRAN & TECH hardware warranty?
All flagship laptops, mobile devices, and displays include a standard 1-Year Comprehensive Manufacturer Hardware Warranty covering logic boards, displays, power supplies, and internal silicon components.
What is your return & exchange policy?
We maintain a 30-day zero-risk return policy for unopened or defect-reported hardware. Returned items undergo automated hardware inspection at our fulfillment laboratory, and refunds are posted within 3-5 business days.
Can I order custom BTO (Build-to-Order) upgrades?
Yes. On each product configurator page, you can choose factory RAM and NVMe SSD expansions (such as 32GB RAM and 2TB SSD). All upgrades are factory-benchmarked and thermal-tested before shipment.
