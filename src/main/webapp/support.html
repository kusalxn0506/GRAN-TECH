Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a secure tracking link. You can also view real-time shipping updates by logging into your Account Dashboard.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day risk-free return policy on all pristine condition hardware. Simply initiate a return from your account portal, and we will provide a prepaid shipping label.
Do your products come with a warranty?
Yes. All GRAN & TECH devices include a standard 1-Year Limited Hardware Warranty. You can upgrade to our extended TechCare+ protection plan during the checkout process.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently, we offer expedited shipping across North America and Europe. We are actively expanding our fulfillment centers to support global distribution by Q4.
